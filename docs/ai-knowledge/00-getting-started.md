# Getting started

This workspace application is a CRM for your organization. You use it to keep track of the
customers you work with, plan meetings in a shared calendar, talk to colleagues in internal
chat, and receive in-app notifications about things that concern you.

## What you can do today

- **Customers** — keep a list of the companies and individuals you do business with, their
  contact persons, owners and statuses (Workspace → "Customers").
- **Calendar** — create events, invite colleagues as guests, answer invitations and set
  personal reminders ("Calendar" in the left rail).
- **Chat** — internal conversations with colleagues of your organization ("Chat" in the left
  rail).
- **Notifications** — an in-app inbox behind the bell icon in the header.
- **Your own settings** — profile, email, password, signed-in devices, notification choices
  and appearance ("User settings" in the account menu).
- **Organization settings** — organization name, currency, calendar defaults and customer
  numbering; available to administrators only.

## Signing in

Open the sign-in page and fill in "Email" and "Password", then click "Sign in". The field
"Organization (optional)" is the sign-in identifier of your organization; fill it in only if
your administrator told you to.

There is no self-registration: nobody can create an account for themselves. An account is
created for you by someone in your organization on the Users page, together with an initial
password. If you do not have an account, ask your administrator.

If you forgot your password, click "Forgot password?" on the sign-in page. See "Account and
security" for the full flow.

## Roles

Every person has one of two roles:

- **User** (`USER`) — the regular role.
- **Admin** (`ADMIN`) — can additionally open the "Settings" area and change organization-wide
  settings.

You can see your role on the Dashboard ("Role" card) and in User settings → "Account". Only
someone who manages accounts on the Users page can change a role.

## Your first steps

1. Open **User settings → Profile** and fill in your "Name" and, if it differs from the
   organization default, your "Time zone". Your calendar is drawn in this zone.
2. Look at the **Dashboard** (Workspace → "Dashboard"): it shows who you are signed in as, your
   role, since when you are a member, and your "Upcoming Events".
3. Open **Customers** to see the organization's customer list.
4. Open **Calendar** to see your week.

## Things that do not exist

To avoid searching for them: there are no tickets, no billing or invoices, no import or export
of data, no bulk actions, no recurring calendar events, no email notifications (notifications
are in-app only), no two-factor authentication, and no mobile app. The interface is available
in English only.
