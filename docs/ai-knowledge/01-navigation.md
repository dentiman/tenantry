# Navigation and layout

The screen is split into columns, from left to right.

## 1. The icon rail (far left)

A narrow column of icons, one per **area** of the application. Hover an icon to see its name.
The areas, top to bottom:

| Area | What is inside |
| --- | --- |
| "Workspace" | "Dashboard", "Users", "Customers" |
| "Calendar" | Day, week, month and agenda views of your events |
| "Chat" | Internal conversations with colleagues |
| "Settings" | Organization settings — shown only to administrators |
| "Playground" | A demo of interface components for developers; nothing here affects your data |
| "Guide" | A one-page overview of the product and its modules |

The logo tile at the top takes you to the Workspace. Clicking the icon of the area you are
already in collapses or expands the second column.

## 2. The area sidebar (second column)

Shows the navigation of the active area. At the top you see your organization's name and the
area name. You can drag its right edge to make it wider.

- In **Workspace** it lists "Dashboard", "Users" and "Customers".
- In **Calendar** it shows a small month picker instead of a menu. If your device's time zone
  differs from your calendar time zone, it warns "Times are shown in …" with a link "Change
  your time zone".
- In **Chat** it lists your conversations.
- In **Settings** (administrators) it lists "General", "Calendar", "Numbering", "Activity".
- In **User settings** it lists "Profile", "Account", "Security", "Notifications",
  "Appearance".

At the bottom of the sidebar is your avatar and name. Click it (the "Account menu") to open
"User settings" or to "Log out".

## 3. The header bar

Above the page content, from left to right:

- a button that hides or shows the area sidebar;
- the page title and breadcrumbs (for example "Customers › Acme Inc");
- the **bell** — your notifications. A badge shows how many new notifications arrived since
  you last opened the panel;
- the **theme switcher** ("Switch theme": "Light", "Dark", "System");
- the **assistant button** ("Toggle assistant") — opens this AI assistant; it is present only
  when the assistant is enabled in your installation;
- a **context panel** button ("Show context panel" / "Hide context panel") — appears only when
  the current page has something to show in the context panel.

## 4. The context panel

A column on the right that shows the details of one item next to the page instead of on top
of it. Today it is used for **calendar events**: opening an event in the Calendar, in the
"Upcoming" card of a customer, or in the "Upcoming Events" widget of the Dashboard shows the
event in this panel. It has its own close button and can be resized.

Customers and users open as full pages, not in the context panel.

## 5. The assistant panel

The AI assistant opens as a column at the far right, next to (not instead of) the context
panel. It stays open while you move between areas, so you can keep asking while you work.

## Other surfaces

- The **notification panel** slides in from the right when you click the bell.
- The **Activity** drawer slides in from the right when you choose "View activity" on a row of
  the Users list.

## Page not found

If you open a wrong link you see "This page does not exist" with the buttons "Go back" and
"Back to home".

## Not available

There is no command palette and no global search across the application; search is
available inside individual lists (customers, users, chat conversations).
