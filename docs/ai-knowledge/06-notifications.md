# Notifications

Notifications are in-app only: they appear in the application, never by email or phone.

## The bell

The bell in the header opens the notification panel. The badge on the bell counts
notifications that arrived **since you last opened the panel** (up to "99+"); opening the
panel clears the badge. A notification stays **unread** until you open it or mark it read.

New notifications also pop up briefly in the corner of the screen while the panel is closed
("Dismiss" hides the pop-up).

## The panel

- Tabs: "Unread", "All" (default) and "Archived".
- Click a notification to mark it read and go to the user, customer, event or chat conversation it is about.
- The row menu ("Notification actions") offers "Mark as read" / "Mark as unread" and "Archive"
  / "Restore". Archiving removes a notification from "All" without deleting it.
- "Mark all as read" at the bottom marks everything read; "Load more" shows older ones.
- The gear ("Notification settings") opens the same switches as User settings →
  "Notifications".
- Empty tabs say "You're all caught up", "No notifications yet" or "Nothing archived".

## What you are notified about

You are never notified about your own actions.

| Setting name | When you get it | Can be turned off |
| --- | --- | --- |
| "New users in the organization" | Someone created a user account (administrators only) | yes |
| "Welcome message" | Your account was created | yes |
| "Customers assigned to me" | You became the owner of a customer | yes |
| "Invitations to events" | You were invited to an event, or a cancelled event was restored | yes |
| "Event time changes" | An event you are invited to was moved | yes |
| "Replies to my events" | A guest answered an event you organize | yes |
| "Event reminders" | A reminder you set is due ("… starts soon") | yes |
| "New chat messages" | A colleague wrote in a conversation you are in while you were **not** looking at it | yes |
| "Password changes" | Your password was changed | no — "Always on" |
| "Event cancellations" | An event you are invited to was cancelled | no — "Always on" |
| "Sign-ins from new devices" | Someone signed in to your account from a new device | no — "Always on" |
| "Suspicious session activity" | Your session may have been copied | no — "Always on" |

Repeated notifications of the same kind (for example several changes to one event, or several
messages in one conversation) are combined into one entry while it is unread — a chat entry then
reads "N new messages". You do not get a chat notification for a conversation you have open at that
moment. Opening a conversation and reading it marks its chat notification read, on every device
where you are signed in. The switches are grouped by category; chat messages are under "Chat".

## Choosing what you receive

User settings → **"Notifications"** — "Choose what lands in your notification inbox." Flip the
switch next to a type. Security-related types show "Always on" and cannot be turned off.

## Not available

No email or push notifications, no snoozing, no digest, and no per-customer or per-event
subscriptions.
