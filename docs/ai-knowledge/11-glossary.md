# Glossary

- **Organization** — your company's space in the application. All customers, events, chats
  and users belong to one organization and are invisible to other organizations.
- **Sign-in identifier** — the short name of your organization typed into "Organization
  (optional)" on the sign-in page.
- **User** — a person who can sign in to your organization. Also the name of the regular role.
- **Admin** — the role that can open the "Settings" area and change organization settings.
- **Area** — one section of the application, chosen in the icon rail on the left: "Workspace",
  "Calendar", "Chat", "Settings", "Playground", "Guide".
- **Icon rail** — the narrow column of area icons at the far left.
- **Sidebar** — the second column, with the navigation of the active area.
- **Context panel** — the column on the right that shows the details of one item (today, a
  calendar event) next to the page.
- **Assistant panel** — the right-most column with this AI assistant.
- **Dashboard** — the start page of the Workspace.
- **Customer** — a company ("Company") or a private person ("Individual") you do business
  with.
- **Contact (contact person)** — a person you talk to at a customer. One can be the "Primary
  contact".
- **Owner** — the colleague responsible for a customer.
- **Customer number** — the human-readable number of a customer, such as `C-000001`; assigned
  automatically, never changed or reused.
- **Status** — for customers: "Active", "Inactive" or "Archived".
- **Archive** — hide a customer from the default list without deleting it.
- **Event** — an entry in the calendar: a meeting, call, deadline, time out of office, or
  other.
- **Organizer** — the person who created an event and the only one who can change it.
- **Guest** — a colleague invited to an event.
- **RSVP** — a guest's answer to an invitation: "Yes" ("Going"), "Maybe" or "No" ("Declined").
- **Reminder** — a personal in-app notification before an event starts.
- **Private event** — an event linked to a customer that is not shown to the whole
  organization.
- **All-day event** — an event that covers whole days rather than a time range.
- **Time zone** — the zone your calendar is drawn in; yours in "Profile", or the
  organization's default.
- **Notification** — an in-app message in the bell panel; can be read, unread or archived.
- **Session** — one signed-in browser or device; listed under "Active sessions".
- **Conversation** — a chat between colleagues. The organization's default channel includes
  everyone.
- **Activity** — the history of important changes to a customer, user, event or the settings.
- **Numbering** — the settings that define how customer numbers look.
- **Assistant** — this AI help: it answers questions about how to use the product and has no
  access to your data.
