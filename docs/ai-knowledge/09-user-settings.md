# User settings

Your personal settings. Open them from the account menu at the bottom of the sidebar (your
avatar and name) → **"User settings"**. The sections are "Profile", "Account", "Security",
"Notifications" and "Appearance". Every user has them, whatever the role.

## Profile

"This is how you appear to other people in this organization."

- "Avatar" — "Change" or "Remove" your picture (JPEG, PNG, WebP or GIF, under 10 MB).
- "Name" — "Your full name".
- "Phone".
- "Language" — only "English"; "Language of the emails we send you."
- "Time zone" — an IANA name such as `Europe/Kyiv`. "Leave empty to follow the organization
  default. Your calendar is drawn in this zone."

Click "Save changes" ("Profile updated").

## Account

"Facts about your account, and how to change your email."

- Your "Email" (read-only here), "Role" (`ADMIN` or `USER`), "Status", "Member since" and
  "Last seen".
- "Change email" — see "Account security, passwords and devices".

You cannot change your own role or status; ask someone who manages accounts.

## Security

"Password" (change your password) and "Active sessions" (see and sign out your devices). See
"Account security, passwords and devices".

## Notifications

"Choose what lands in your notification inbox." One switch per notification type; types
marked "Always on" cannot be turned off. See "Notifications".

## Appearance

"How this app looks on this device. The choice is stored in this browser only."

- "Theme" — "Light", "Dark" or "System" (follows your operating system). The same choice is
  available from the theme switcher in the header.
- "Color theme" — "Dark grey" or "Slate"; "Applies to both light and dark mode."

Because the appearance is stored in the browser, another browser or device keeps its own
choice.

## Not available

No interface language other than English, no date or number format choice, and no email
notification preferences (notifications are in-app only).
